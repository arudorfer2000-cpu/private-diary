## Facts that shape your options
- **UK:** Austrian and Spanish citizens pay the **international** fee (since Brexit) and are not eligible for UK "Home" bursaries. Fees are about £31,400 for Manchester's MA Arts Management in 2027 (search-confirmed). Funding is therefore the deciding factor for a UK Master's.
- **Ireland:** EU citizens pay the lower **EU fee** (TCD History of Art MPhil about €8,000 per year vs €16,500 international, from the 2026 page). Irish Research Council funding may require 3 of the last 5 years living in the EU/EEA, which you can likely meet.
- **UK PhDs:** The AHRC is cutting funded places by almost a third (search-confirmed). Several Doctoral Landscape Award schemes now accept international students at full rates, but competition is high.
- **Timing:** Most 2027-entry deadlines fall between **November 2026 and January 2027**, so the key dates are weeks away.

## C. Home-country funders (Austria and Spain)
These are the best matches to your citizenships. I did not confirm any 2027 detail, so verify each one.
- **la Caixa Foundation**, postgraduate fellowships abroad (Spanish citizens). Historically the strongest Spanish award for study in the UK. Calls usually open in winter.
- **Fundación Ramón Areces**, postgraduate study abroad, social sciences (Spanish citizens).
- **Fundación Rafael del Pino** and **Banco de España**, mostly economics (Spanish citizens).
- **OeAD / grants.at**, database of Austrian outbound funding, including the Marietta Blau grant for doctoral research abroad.
- **Austrian Studienbeihilfe**, Auslandsstipendium, for study abroad if you meet the means test.
- **Erasmus Mundus MAGMa** (Managing Art and Cultural Heritage in Global Markets, with Glasgow as a partner). Found only for 2026 entry. The 2027 call would likely close about mid-January 2027.
